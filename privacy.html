Privacy Policy

Nunco · Last updated 26 October 2026

Nunco does not collect, store or transmit any personal information to its developer. There is no account to create, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party tracking of any kind.

Location

If you allow it, Nunco uses your device's location for two things: to fetch the weather where you are, and to show the name of that place.

  • Your coordinates are sent to Apple's weather service to retrieve a forecast.
  • Your coordinates are passed to Apple's mapping services to turn them into a place name, and to search for cities when you add one.

That is the full extent of it. Your location is never sent to the developer, never stored on any server belonging to this app, and never sold or shared with anyone else. Granting location access is optional — the app works without it, and you can add cities by name instead.

What stays on your device

The cities you save, the note you write, and your settings are stored on your iPhone only. They are not backed up to any service run by this app, and deleting the app deletes them.

What the app does not do

  • No user accounts, sign-ins or profiles.
  • No analytics, crash reporting or usage measurement.
  • No advertising, and no advertising identifiers.
  • No third-party software development kits of any kind.
  • Nothing is sold, rented or shared with data brokers.

Third-party services

The forecast, the place names and the city search all come from Apple, through Apple Weather and Apple's mapping services. Each request carries only the coordinates or the search text needed to answer it, and nothing that identifies you or your device. Apple's handling of that is covered by Apple's privacy policy.

Children

Nunco collects no personal information from anyone, of any age.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date at the top.
