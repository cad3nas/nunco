Nunco Support

A quiet weather app for iPhone — today's conditions, the week ahead, and a note for whatever the day needs.

Need help, found a bug, or want to suggest something?
Email nuncoapp@gmail.com. Messages are read by the person who made the app, and replied to.

Using the app

Adding a city
Tap the button in the corner and choose Add a city, then type a name. Swipe left and right to move between the places you have saved.
Today or the week
Double-tap the bar at the bottom to switch between today's readings and the next few days.
Refreshing
Pull down anywhere on the page. The mark in the middle turns while it fetches.
The note
Opened from the same button. It saves as you type and appears under the temperature on the main screen.
Left or right handed
The floating button and the panels sit in whichever bottom corner suits you. Change it in Settings.

Common questions

Why does it ask for my location?
To show the weather where you are and to name that place. It is optional — decline it and the app works normally, you just add cities by name instead. Nothing about your location is sent to the developer. See the privacy policy.
I declined location and want to turn it on
iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services → Nunco.
A city I searched for was not found
Check the connection and try the city with its region, for example "Chelsea, London". If a search keeps failing on a working connection, email and say which name you typed.
Where does the weather come from?
Apple Weather. The Apple Weather mark sits at the foot of every page in the app and links to Apple's list of the data sources behind it.
Larger text and VoiceOver
Both are supported throughout. If something is cut off or read oddly at your text size, that is a bug worth reporting.
